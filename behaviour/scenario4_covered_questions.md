# Scenario 4 — Wrong Goal Discovered Late (Order execution)

Scenario summary

During an order-execution bug fix, after being roughly 75% through the work, I realized we had been operating on the wrong assumption about an asset ID. The logs were misleading due to the incorrect asset reference. I informed stakeholders, extended the timeline, revisited the correct logs, and fixed the actual issue.

Full STAR answer

S (Situation): While fixing an order-execution bug, we were about 75% through the change when I discovered the original assumption about the asset ID being used was incorrect; the logs were pointing us away from the real problem.

T (Task): Ensure we fixed the correct root cause, communicate impact, and deliver a working fix even if the timeline had to shift.

A (Action): I paused further non-essential work, validated the correct asset ID with the owner, updated our logging filters, informed stakeholders with a new timeline, and focused the team on the correct root-cause analysis.

R (Result): We delivered the corrected fix after the timeline extension, the underlying issue was resolved, and stakeholder trust was maintained due to transparent communication.
